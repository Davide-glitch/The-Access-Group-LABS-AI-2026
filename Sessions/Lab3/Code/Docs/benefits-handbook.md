# Benefits Handbook — Acme Software Ltd

**Owner:** People & Culture | **Effective:** 1 January 2026

---

## Overview

Acme offers a comprehensive benefits package designed to support your health, finances, and wellbeing. Unless stated otherwise, benefits are available to all permanent employees from their start date.

You must enrol in benefits within **30 days of joining** via the Benefits Portal at **benefits.acme.com**. After this window, changes can only be made during the annual benefits review in October, or following a qualifying life event.

---

## Private Medical Insurance

**Provider:** AXA Health  
**Cover level:** Core (employee) by default; family cover available at subsidised cost.

Core cover includes:
- Inpatient and day-patient treatment at approved hospitals
- Outpatient consultations (up to £1,000 per year)
- Physiotherapy (up to 10 sessions per year with GP referral)
- Mental health support (up to 8 sessions per year)
- Cancer care (full cover, no limit)
- Optical: up to £200 every 2 years
- Dental: up to £300 per year for routine treatment

To add a partner or dependants, log in to benefits.acme.com and select **Add Family Member**. The additional premium will be deducted from your monthly salary. Family cover starts on the first day of the month following enrolment.

To make a claim or book a specialist appointment, call AXA Health on **0800 000 111** or use the AXA Health app. You typically do not need a GP referral for an initial specialist consultation under this policy.

## Pension

**Scheme:** Acme Group Pension (defined contribution)  
**Provider:** Nest Pensions

Contribution rates:
| | Percentage of qualifying earnings |
|---|---|
| **Employee minimum** | 5% |
| **Employer contribution** | 8% |
| **Total minimum** | 13% |

Employees may increase their contribution at any time via the Benefits Portal. Employer contributions do not increase above 8% but Acme will match any additional employee contributions up to an additional 2% (i.e. up to 10% employer total for employees contributing 7%+).

All contributions are made via salary sacrifice, which reduces your National Insurance liability. Your pension statement is available at nest.co.uk using your personal Nest account.

## Wellbeing Allowance

Every employee receives **£500 per year** to spend on physical or mental wellbeing activities. This is paid as a taxable benefit and processed through payroll quarterly (£125 per quarter).

Eligible spend includes:
- Gym memberships and fitness classes
- Sports equipment (e.g. running shoes, yoga mat)
- Mental health apps (e.g. Calm, Headspace)
- Therapy or counselling sessions not covered by AXA
- Meditation or mindfulness courses
- Nutrition or sleep coaching

Ineligible spend: alcohol, tobacco, recreational drugs, general clothing.

Submit receipts via the HR Portal under **Wellbeing Claim** within 3 months of purchase. Your quarterly payment will include a lump sum for approved claims received in that quarter.

## Employee Assistance Programme (EAP)

**Provider:** Health Assured  
**Available to:** All employees and their immediate household members (partner, dependants)  
**Cost:** Free

The EAP provides confidential support 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year:

- **Counselling:** Up to 8 free sessions with a qualified counsellor (telephone, video, or face-to-face)
- **Legal advice:** Free 30-minute consultations on personal legal matters (employment, family, consumer)
- **Financial guidance:** Help with budgeting, debt, and financial planning
- **Life events support:** Bereavement, relationship difficulties, caring responsibilities

Call: **0800 028 0199** (free, 24/7)  
Online portal: **healthassured.org/acme** (access code: ACME2026)

All contacts with the EAP are **completely confidential**. Acme does not receive any information about individual employees' use of the service.

## Life Assurance

**Cover:** 4× annual basic salary, paid as a tax-free lump sum to your nominated beneficiary.

To nominate a beneficiary, complete the **Expression of Wishes** form in the Benefits Portal. You may nominate multiple beneficiaries with percentage splits. Review your nomination whenever your circumstances change (marriage, divorce, birth of a child).

Cover ceases at age 70 or on leaving Acme employment.

## Cycle to Work Scheme

Acme participates in the **Cyclescheme** programme. You can spread the cost of a bike and equipment (helmet, lights, lock) over 12 months via salary sacrifice, saving up to 42% on the retail price.

- Maximum spend: **£2,500** per application
- You can apply once per 12-month period
- Coverage: bikes, e-bikes, and all safety equipment
- The bike must be used primarily for commuting

To apply, visit **cyclescheme.co.uk/acme** and choose your bike and retailer. Cyclescheme will send a voucher within 3–5 working days.

## Season Ticket Loan

Acme offers an interest-free loan of up to **£5,000** for an annual public transport season ticket. The loan is repaid monthly through payroll over the term of the season ticket.

To apply, raise a request through the HR Portal at least 2 weeks before your season ticket renewal date. Loans are subject to passing probation and a minimum 6-month remaining tenure.
