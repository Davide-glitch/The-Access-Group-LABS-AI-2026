# Expense Policy — Acme Software Ltd

**Version:** 3.1 | **Effective:** 1 March 2026 | **Owner:** Finance

---

## Overview

Acme will reimburse reasonable, necessary business expenses incurred wholly and exclusively in the performance of your role. All expenses must be submitted through the **ExpenseTrack** system (expenses.acme.com) within **30 days** of incurring the cost. Claims submitted after 30 days require Finance Director approval and may be declined.

Personal expenses — including alcohol purchased for personal consumption, gifts unrelated to business development, or personal travel — will not be reimbursed.

## Submitting an Expense Claim

1. Log in to ExpenseTrack at expenses.acme.com using your company SSO credentials.
2. Click **New Claim** and select the appropriate expense category.
3. Enter the date, supplier, business purpose, and amount in GBP.
4. Attach a **receipt or VAT invoice** for every item over £10.
5. For meals with clients or colleagues, name all attendees and the business purpose.
6. Submit the claim — your line manager will receive a notification to approve it.
7. Approved claims are processed in the next payroll run (typically within 2 weeks).

All claims must be submitted in **British Pounds (GBP)**. ExpenseTrack will automatically convert foreign currency receipts using the HMRC monthly exchange rate.

## Spending Limits

### Accommodation

| Location | Nightly limit |
|---|---|
| London | £180 per night |
| Other UK cities | £130 per night |
| European cities | £160 per night |
| Rest of world | £200 per night |

If a suitable hotel within the limit is unavailable (e.g. during a major conference), you must obtain written pre-approval from your manager before booking. Include the pre-approval email when submitting your claim.

### Meals and Subsistence

| Scenario | Limit |
|---|---|
| Working away (no overnight stay) | £15 for lunch, £30 for dinner |
| Overnight stay — daily subsistence | £50 per day total |
| Client entertainment (per person) | £75 per person |

Subsistence is only claimable when you are more than 5 miles from your normal place of work and away for more than 5 hours.

### Travel

**Rail:** Book standard class. First class requires VP or above approval except for journeys over 3 hours, where standard class premium seating (e.g. Quiet Coach with table) is acceptable.

**Air travel:** Economy class for all flights up to 6 hours. Business class requires VP approval. Always book through the company's preferred travel agent (Clyde Travel) to capture negotiated rates.

**Car mileage:** If using your own vehicle for a business journey, claim at **45p per mile** for the first 10,000 miles in the tax year, and **25p per mile** thereafter, in line with HMRC approved mileage rates.

**Taxis:** Taxis are acceptable when public transport is unavailable, unsafe, or impractical given luggage or time constraints. Uber receipts are accepted; ensure the receipt shows the route.

**Parking:** Reasonable parking costs at meetings or airports are reimbursable. Parking fines and congestion charges are not reimbursable.

### Telecoms

Company mobile phones are provided to employees who require them. Personal phone calls on a company device should be repaid to the company. Reasonable roaming data costs incurred on company business are reimbursable with a receipt.

### Equipment

Small business equipment (e.g. a mouse for a hotel stay) up to £25 may be expensed without pre-approval. Equipment over £25 should be procured through IT to ensure asset registration.

## Approval Thresholds

| Claim total | Required approver |
|---|---|
| Under £100 | Direct line manager |
| £100–£499 | Direct line manager (countersigned by Finance) |
| £500–£1,999 | Senior Manager + Finance Business Partner |
| £2,000 and above | Finance Director |

Client entertainment claims always require VP approval regardless of amount.

## Receipts

Original receipts (or clear photographs/scans) are required for all expenses over £10. Missing receipts may result in the claim being rejected. If a receipt is lost, complete a **Missing Receipt Declaration** form in ExpenseTrack and have it countersigned by your manager.

VAT receipts (showing the supplier's VAT number and the VAT breakdown) are required for claims where VAT reclaim is applicable (most hotel, rail, and supplier invoices).

## Non-Reimbursable Items

The following are **not** reimbursable under any circumstances:
- Alcohol purchased for personal consumption (alcohol included in a business dinner is covered under the entertainment limit)
- Personal items such as toiletries, clothing, or gifts for personal use
- Fines, penalties, or legal fees
- Gym or leisure expenses (see Benefits Handbook for the Wellbeing Allowance)
- Travel between home and normal place of work (standard commuting)

## Queries
