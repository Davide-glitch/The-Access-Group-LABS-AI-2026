# Leave Policy — Acme Software Ltd

**Version:** 4.2 | **Effective:** 1 January 2026 | **Owner:** People & Culture

---

## Annual Leave Entitlement

All permanent employees receive a minimum of **25 days** of paid annual leave per year, in addition to all UK public and bank holidays (usually 8 days). Employees on fixed-term contracts of six months or more receive the same entitlement on a pro-rated basis.

Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis calculated against a standard 37.5-hour working week. For example, an employee working 3 days per week receives 15 days per year.

Your leave entitlement is stated in your contract of employment. Some roles may carry a higher entitlement as part of a compensation package.

## Holiday Year

The holiday year runs from **1 January to 31 December**. Entitlement cannot be rolled over except as described in the Carry-Over section below.

## How to Book Leave

All leave must be requested and approved in advance through the **HR Portal** (portal.acme.com). To book leave:

1. Log in to the HR Portal using your company email and single sign-on.
2. Navigate to **My Leave > Request Leave**.
3. Select the leave type, start date, and end date.
4. Add a brief note if the timing is sensitive.
5. Submit — your line manager will receive an email notification to approve or decline.

Leave is not confirmed until your manager has approved it in the portal. Verbal agreements do not constitute approval. You will receive an email confirmation once approved.

## Carry-Over

You may carry over a maximum of **5 days** of unused annual leave into the following holiday year. Carried-over days must be used by **31 March** of the new year or they are forfeited. Exceptional carry-over requests (e.g. long-term sickness during the leave year) may be approved by your HR Business Partner.

## Sick Leave

Acme operates a generous sick pay policy:

| Duration of illness | Pay |
|---|---|
| Days 1–3 | Full pay (self-certificated) |
| Days 4–7 | Full pay (self-certificated) |
| Day 8 onwards | Full pay, medical certificate required |
| After 4 weeks continuous absence | Occupational sick pay reviewed by HR |

For absences of 4 or more consecutive days (including weekends), you must provide a fit note from a GP or equivalent medical practitioner. Fit notes should be submitted to your line manager and HR within 2 working days of issue.

To report sickness, call or message your line manager **before your normal start time** on the first day of absence. Do not send an email as your primary notification — a direct message or call is required.

## Parental Leave

### Maternity and Primary Carer Leave

Employees who have been with Acme for 12 months or more are entitled to:

- **Weeks 1–26:** Full company pay (in addition to any statutory entitlement)
- **Weeks 27–39:** Statutory Maternity Pay (SMP) only
- **Weeks 40–52:** Unpaid

Employees with fewer than 12 months of service receive statutory entitlements only.

### Paternity / Secondary Carer Leave

2 weeks of paid paternity leave may be taken within 56 days of the birth or adoption placement. Additional unpaid leave up to a total of 4 weeks may be agreed with your manager.

### Shared Parental Leave

Both parents may share up to 50 weeks of leave and up to 37 weeks of pay. Contact your HR Business Partner to discuss your options at least 8 weeks before your intended start date.

## Compassionate and Emergency Leave

Up to **3 days** of paid compassionate leave per incident is available for the death of an immediate family member (spouse/partner, parent, child, sibling). Additional unpaid leave may be agreed with your manager for travel or caring responsibilities.

Emergency leave for dependant care (e.g. unexpected childcare breakdown, a dependant becoming suddenly ill) is available at the manager's discretion on a case-by-case basis.

## Unpaid Leave

Requests for unpaid leave of up to 4 weeks may be considered at the manager's discretion, subject to business needs. Requests should be made at least 4 weeks in advance. Longer unpaid leave (e.g. a sabbatical) requires HR and senior management approval and is subject to a minimum of 2 years' service.

## Contact

For leave queries, contact your HR Business Partner or email **hr@acme.com**.
