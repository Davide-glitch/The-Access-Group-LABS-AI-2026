# New Joiner Onboarding Guide — Acme Software Ltd

**Owner:** People & Culture | **Last updated:** January 2026

Welcome to Acme! This guide covers everything you need to know for your first days and weeks.

---

## Before You Start

Your hiring manager should have sent you a welcome email containing:
- Your start date, time, and office location
- Your company email address (you'll set your password on day one)
- A link to complete pre-employment paperwork (Right to Work check, bank details, P45)
- A link to this guide and the IT setup checklist

If you haven't received this by 5 working days before your start date, contact **hr@acme.com**.

---

## Day One

### Arrival

Please arrive by **09:00** on your first day. Our London head office is at:

> Acme Software Ltd  
> 12 Finsbury Pavement  
> London EC2A 1AA

**Reception is on the ground floor.** Ask for your hiring manager or the People & Culture team by name. Bring a valid photo ID (passport, driving licence, or national identity card) — it is needed for the Right to Work check if not already completed remotely.

### Visitor Badge → Staff Badge

You will be given a temporary visitor badge at reception. On your first day, visit **Security (Ground Floor, Room G04)** with your photo ID to collect your permanent staff badge. Your badge gives you access to all standard floors. Restricted areas (server room, finance vault) require a separate access request through the helpdesk.

Register your car registration plate with Security if you wish to use the badge-access car park (Level B1). Parking is on a first-come, first-served basis and availability varies by site.

### IT Equipment

Your laptop and any other equipment will be ready for collection at the **IT Desk (Second Floor, Room 2.12)** on your first morning. You will need to sign for the equipment. IT will walk you through:
- Initial login and MFA setup
- VPN configuration
- Key bookmarks and system access

If anything is missing from your equipment pack, raise a ticket with IT immediately rather than waiting.

## Your First Week

### Your Buddy

You will be assigned an **onboarding buddy** — a colleague in a nearby role who can answer informal questions and help you navigate the culture. Your buddy is not your manager; think of them as a friendly guide for the first 3 months.

Your buddy's name will be in your welcome email. Reach out to them before your start date if you'd like.

### Core Systems

| System | Purpose | Access |
|---|---|---|
| **Slack** | Team messaging and collaboration | Auto-provisioned on day 1 |
| **Jira** | Tickets, sprints, project tracking | Auto-provisioned on day 1 |
| **Confluence** | Internal wiki, documentation, runbooks | Auto-provisioned on day 1 |
| **HR Portal** (portal.acme.com) | Leave, expenses, payslips, personal details | Auto-provisioned on day 1 |
| **GitHub** (github.com/acmesoftware) | Code repositories | IT ticket required — request on day 1 |
| **Salesforce** | CRM (customer-facing roles only) | Raise IT ticket with manager approval |

Your manager will send you a list of team-specific Slack channels to join. The company-wide #general and #announcements channels are joined automatically.

### Your 30-60-90 Plan

Your manager will schedule a 30-60-90 check-in to track your progress:
- **30 days:** Onboarding complete, systems access sorted, first tasks underway
- **60 days:** Contributing to team work, relationships established
- **90 days:** Operating independently, performance conversation with your manager

## Key Policies to Read

During your first week, please read:

- **[Acceptable Use Policy](confluence.acme.com/it/aup)** — what you can and can't do on company systems
- **[Data Protection & GDPR Policy](confluence.acme.com/legal/gdpr)** — how we handle personal data
- **[Code of Conduct](confluence.acme.com/hr/code-of-conduct)** — our behavioural standards
- **[Security Awareness Training](lms.acme.com)** — mandatory 30-minute e-learning module, complete by end of week 2

HR will send you reminders, but please don't wait — completing these early protects both you and the company.

## Benefits Enrolment

You have a **30-day window** from your start date to enrol in the company pension, private medical insurance, and any voluntary benefits. After 30 days, you can only change elections at the annual benefits window (October) or following a qualifying life event (marriage, birth of a child, etc.).

Log in to the **Benefits Portal** at benefits.acme.com using your company SSO to view and select your benefits. See the Benefits Handbook for full details of what's on offer.

---

## Payroll

You will be paid monthly on the **last working day of the month**. Your first payslip will appear in the HR Portal within 3 working days of payment. If you have not submitted your bank details and P45 before your first payroll cut-off (usually the 15th of the month), your first payment may be processed in the following month.

## Office Practicalities

**Working hours:** Core hours are 10:00–16:00. Outside core hours, flexible working is supported for most roles — discuss your preferred pattern with your manager.

**Kitchen:** Free tea, coffee, and fruit are provided on all floors. The subsidised canteen on Floor 1 is open Mon–Fri 07:30–15:00.

**Meeting rooms:** Book via Outlook or the room-booking terminals outside each room. Rooms should be released if not used within 10 minutes of the booking start.

**Post and couriers:** Use the company address for work-related deliveries only. All parcels are logged by reception.

## Questions?

Your first port of call for any question is your **buddy**, then your **manager**. For HR-specific queries, email **hr@acme.com** or drop in to the People & Culture team on Floor 3 (Room 3.01).
